Features

Small nudges. Healthier days.

Everything lives in your menu bar and asks for as little attention as possible.

🧠

Counts real screen time

Lock your Mac, let it sleep or walk away, and the timer starts over. You only get breaks after you've actually been staring at the screen.

📞

Quiet during calls

Mic or camera on, or sharing your screen? Breaks wait until the call ends, then show up a few seconds later.

💧

Water reminders

About once an hour, a small popup with a silly line reminds you to drink. Log a glass with ⌃⌥W and track your daily count.

🧍

Posture nudges

A short, funny reminder to straighten up. Turn it off any time from the menu.

⌨️

Keyboard first

Start, snooze, skip and adjust everything without touching the mouse.

✨

Your own popups

Make reminders for anything: stretch, eye drops, a walk. Pick an icon, colour and interval, all from the keyboard.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no network calls, no special permissions. It all stays on your Mac.

🪶

Tiny and native

A lightweight native Swift app, well under 1 MB, that sips memory and battery.

The science bit

What is the 20-20-20 rule?

Focusing on a close screen keeps your eye muscles tense for hours. Eye doctors recommend a simple habit to let them relax, and Healthy Pops does the remembering for you.

20minutes of screen time
20feet away (about 6 m)
20seconds of looking
FAQ

Questions, answered

What is the 20-20-20 rule?

Every 20 minutes of screen time, look at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away for about 20 seconds. It relaxes the eye muscles that stay tense while focusing on a close screen and helps reduce digital eye strain.

Is Healthy Pops free?

Yes, free forever. It is open source under the MIT license, with no account, ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Will it interrupt my meetings?

No. While your microphone or camera is in use, or you are sharing your screen in Zoom, breaks and reminders wait and appear shortly after the call ends.

Does Healthy Pops collect any data?

No. Everything runs locally on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere and it needs no special permissions.

Why does macOS warn me when I first open it?

The app is not notarized with a paid Apple Developer ID. Right-click Healthy Pops in Applications and choose Open the first time; after that it opens normally.

Can I change the timings?

Yes. Screen time, break length, water and posture intervals can all be adjusted from the menu bar, and water and posture reminders can be switched off.

How can I contribute?

Fork the GitHub repository, run ./build.sh --install to build your copy, make your changes and open a pull request. Ideas and bug reports are welcome as issues too.

Can I make my own reminders?

Yes. Custom Popups in the menu opens a keyboard-friendly editor where you pick a title, message, icon, colour and how often it appears, measured in screen time.
